What Makes Sirona's Checkout Different

The basket is built, the need is urgent, and the purchase is personal

Four things about Sirona's store shape what a checkout should do.

01

Private purchases need a confident pay screen

Intimate and period care is bought carefully. Pay After Delivery and risk-tagged COD let a first-time buyer complete without second-guessing.

02

Baskets are built, not picked

With Buy 2 Get 1 Free live, customers add items to qualify. A smart cart that suggests the right third item lifts the basket at the moment it is being built.

03

Many needs are urgent

A period product or a PeeBuddy for a trip is needed by a date. Showing a delivery date before add-to-cart answers the question that decides the sale.

04

Four brands, one cart

A customer can add a cup, a travel urination device and a condom pack in one order. One address, one payment flow, one delivery promise.

Part Two — Engage

What happens after the order is where repeat customers are made

Sirona's best products are bought again: pads and washes every month, a sterilizer with a cup, a PeeBuddy for the next trip. Engage 360 is how that repeat purchase is earned, across WhatsApp, Instagram, email, SMS, RCS and voice.

Core

Growth Manager

A dedicated fastrr growth manager plans campaigns and journeys with your team, so Engage is run with you, not left as a tool.

WhatsApp

Marketing and utility messages

Offers like Buy 2 Get 1 Free go out as marketing messages. Order confirmations, shipping updates and delivery alerts go out as utility messages. Customer replies inside a session are free.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp chatbot

Answers product and order questions on WhatsApp, around the clock, and hands off to your team when a person should step in.

Instagram

Instagram chatbot

Replies to comments and DMs instantly, so interest from Sirona's content turns into a conversation and then an order.

Reach

Anonymous users

Visitors who never logged in are recognised and brought back into journeys, so a browsing session on a cup or sterilizer does not end as a lost visit.

Channels

Email, SMS and RCS

The right channel for the right message: email for long content, SMS for reach, RCS for rich cards with images and buttons.

Voice

AI Calling

Automated calls to confirm a COD order or recover a high-value cart, with the customer's answer fed straight back into the order.

Conversational

AI Assist chatbot

A shopping assistant on the site that guides a customer to the right product, answers questions and adds to cart inside the chat.

  • 60–70% of tickets deflected
  • 80% faster first response
  • 50–60% lower support cost per order
Protection

RTO Suite

Pre-shipment confirmation, address verification and NDR recovery on WhatsApp and voice, to cut returns before the parcel leaves.

  • Up to 45% RTO reduction
